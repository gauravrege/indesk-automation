## What Was Done

I kicked off my internship by modeling a massive 9,090-row raw data dump from the InDesk portal into a live, readable financial dashboard in Google Sheets. I collaborated with Deepa mam, Ajay, and Phani sir to understand the business logic behind Collections vs Dues across 14 distinct geographic regions.

## Technical Execution

- **Live Data Aggregation:** Built complex Google Sheets formulas (`QUERY`, `FILTER`, `SUMIFS`) to automatically aggregate a 9,000+ row raw "Outstanding Report" into readable metrics.
- **Top-Level KPI Cards:** Designed dynamic summary cards to highlight Total Collections (using rolling date ranges), Outstanding Dues, Net Balance, and Collection Coverage percentages.
- **Zonal Navigation & Filtering:** Built interactive filtering mechanisms so management can easily slice the financial data between East, North, South, and West geographical zones.
- **UI/UX Design:** Engineered a clean interface directly within Google Sheets, abandoning the standard raw grid look for a clean, dashboard-style presentation using custom borders, frozen rows, and color-coded conditional formatting.

## Impact

> By structuring the raw Outstanding Report data, I transformed an unreadable data dump into a modern financial dashboard, giving management instant, actionable insights into live cash flows and regional performance.
